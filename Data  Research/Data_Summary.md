## Data Summary

**1. Labour Force Characteristics**
* _Source_: Statistics Canada 
* _Format_: CSV (StatCan Download/API) 
* _Time Range_: 2021 – Present 
* _Update Frequency_: Monthly
* _Granularity_: Province/Territory + Demographics (Sex, Age Group)
* _Key Fields_: Ref_Date, Geography, Labour Force Status (Employed, Unemployed, Participation Rate), Sex, Age Group, Value (Count or Rate)
* _Purpose_: Track labour market participation, unemployment, and employment rates by demographic and region to assess workforce dynamics.

**2. Employment by Industry**
* _Source_: Statistics Canada
* _Format_: CSV (StatCan Download)
* _Time Range_: 1997 – Present
* _Update Frequency_: Monthly
* _Granularity_: Province/Territory + Industry (NAICS)
* _Key Fields_: Ref_Date, Geography, NAICS Industry Classification, Sex, Age Group, Employment (Number)
* _Purpose_: Analyze employment levels over time by region and industry to identify economic growth sectors, job shifts, and regional labor trends.

**3. Projections of Job Openings**
* _Source_: Open Canada
* _Format_: CSV
* _Time Range_: 2023 – 2033
* _Update Frequency_: Periodic 
* _Granularity_: Province/Territory + Industry (NAICS)
* _Key Fields_: Occupation (NOC Code), Expansion Demand, Replacement Demand, Total Job Openings, Projected Employment
* _Purpose_: Forecast future workforce needs across occupations, helping identify long-term labor shortages or surpluses to support career planning, education strategies, and policy development.

**4. Assessment of Recent Labour Market Conditions by Occupation**
* _Source_: Open Canada
* _Format_: CSV
* _Time Range_: 2021 – 2023
* _Update Frequency_: Annual
* _Granularity_: Province/Territory + Occupation (NOC)
* _Key Fields_: Occupation (NOC Code), Labour Market Status (Shortage, Surplus, Balance)
* _Purpose_: Evaluate short-term labour market imbalances by occupation to inform staffing decisions, talent pipeline strategies, and responsive training initiatives.

**5. Live Job Postings**
* _Source_: Job Bank Canada
* _Format_: JSON / Scraped CSV
* _Time Range_: Real-time / Continuous
* _Update Frequency_: Daily or on-demand scraping
* _Granularity_: Location + Occupation + Employer
* _Key Fields_: Job Title, Location, Posting Date, Salary Range, Job Type (Full-time/Part-time)
* _Purpose_: Provide up-to-date visibility into actual hiring needs across Canada, supporting real-time analytics, trend monitoring, and validation of labour demand signals.
