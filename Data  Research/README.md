## 📊 Data Research - Canadian Job Market Trends Analysis Project

### 🧩 Problem statement

Canada's labor market is undergoing continuous transformation influenced by economic shifts, emerging industries, regional demands, and evolving workforce skills. However, navigating these trends remains complex due to fragmented data sources, inconsistent formats, and rapidly changing job landscapes. This project aims to centralize, clean, and analyze this data to reveal key trends across industries, occupations, regions, and time.

### 📁 Datasets considered

We have collected quite a lot of datasets from multiple sources to understand labor market conditions across time, industries, and geographies. After careful observation, we finalized the following key datasets to support our project objectives:

#### 1. Statistics Canada – Labour Force Characteristics
_https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410028703_
**Description**: Monthly labour force characteristics for the Canadian population from January 2021 to present, including metrics such as employment and unemployment rates by province, sex, and age group.

#### 2. Statistics Canada – Employment by Industry
_https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410002301_
**Description**: Monthly employment data by industry and province, based on NAICS classification. Covers employment trends across multiple sectors, supporting industry-level comparisons and time-series analysis.

#### 3. Open Canada – Projections of Job Openings
_https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/e80851b8-de68-43bd-a85c-c72e1b3a3890/resource/da1135c5-a1df-4a07-a81e-de308ae6cce6_
**Description**: Forward-looking projections estimating job openings by occupation across Canada from 2023 to 2033. This data helps anticipate future workforce needs based on employment growth, retirements, and other labor market dynamics.

#### 4. Open Canada – Assessment of Recent Labour Market Conditions by Occupation
_https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/e80851b8-de68-43bd-a85c-c72e1b3a3890/resource/ff795791-0728-4dcf-8fdd-d7ba9e1210b8_
**Description**: Evaluation of labour market conditions for individual occupations from 2021 to 2023, identifying whether there was a surplus, shortage, or balance in workforce availability across Canadian regions.

#### 5. Job Bank Canada – Live Job Postings
_https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/jobsearch/_
**Description**: Continuously updated repository of active job openings across Canada, providing real-time insights into hiring trends by industry, region, and occupation.

These datasets serve as the foundation for modeling trends, demand forecasting, and dashboard visualizations in the `job_market_trends` system.
